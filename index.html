Flow / Step by step

Reporting a lost item, step by step

From opening the site to the item finding its owner — five steps, no account required

Step 01

Open the lost & found tab

Open the site and hit the “Lost & found” tab at the top. There is nothing to sign into.

Step 02

Say whether it is lost or found

Press the status button that matches your report — the colour changes with your choice.

Step 03

Fill in the details, add a photo

Your name, the item, where, when, and how to reach you. A photo makes it far easier for someone to recognise.

Step 04

Submit — it hits the board instantly

The post appears for everyone with the site open. Tap any card to read it full screen.

Step 05

Owner found — close it out

Press “Owner found” to close the report. Pressed it by mistake? You can undo. Delete, though, is permanent — there is no recovery.
